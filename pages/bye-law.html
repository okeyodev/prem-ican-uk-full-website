Governing Document

ICAN Bye Law for District Societies.

The UK District is governed by the ICAN Bye Law for District Societies. This Bye Law sets out the framework for governance, membership, executive responsibilities, standing committees and the relationship with the Institute.

It ensures consistency with the Chartered Accountants Act 1965 and provides the District with a clear mandate to operate, coordinate affairs, regulate membership and serve the interests of Chartered Accountants in the United Kingdom.
