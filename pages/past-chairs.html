Archival Record

A living archive of leadership and continuity.

From Mrs Camilla Shittu's founding chairmanship in 1990 to Mr Iliyasu Maisanda's Immediate Past Chair tenure in 2023-2024, this archive documents those who have steered the ICAN UK & District Society through three decades of professional service.

Each entry preserves tenure, professional designation and role. Where portrait photography is available, it is displayed; where not, a brass institutional avatar ensures the record remains dignified and complete — ready for future image addition without redesign.
