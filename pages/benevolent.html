Benevolent Fund

A Community That Stands Together

The Benevolent Fund is the welfare arm of ICAN UK & District Society, established to provide practical support to members and their families during periods of difficulty.

It reflects a simple professional principle: belonging to ICAN UK means belonging to a community that looks after its own. The Fund operates with discretion, empathy and institutional responsibility — ensuring that no member has to face hardship alone.

Why the Fund Exists

Professional success is rarely linear. The Fund exists for the moments in between.

Chartered Accountants uphold high standards of professional life, but life itself can present unforeseen challenges — health concerns, family circumstances, or periods of financial strain. The Benevolent Fund was created to ensure practical help is available without compromising professional dignity.

It is not a commercial product or public grant scheme. It is a community welfare commitment, funded and governed by the District, and delivered with care.

Dignity • Confidentiality

Support should be approached with discretion and respect.

Applications to the Benevolent Fund are treated with the utmost confidentiality. Information is shared only with those directly involved in considering support, in accordance with data protection and the Fund's governance.

The process is designed to protect professional dignity at every stage — from initial enquiry to outcome. You will not be asked to share more than is necessary, and your privacy is central to how the Fund operates.
